The Grace of Asking

Dedication

A word of grace for those who give, receive, and learn to ask.

Dedication

To everyone who has ever felt guilty for asking, uncomfortable receiving, or afraid of becoming a burden to someone they love.

To those who apologize before they even make a request, who give freely but struggle to receive, and who have learned to carry more than they were ever meant to carry alone.

May you discover that asking does not make you weak, receiving does not make you selfish, and extending grace does not require you to abandon yourself.

And to the people who love us enough to help without making us feel indebted, may we never take their kindness for granted.

Above all, to Jesus Christ, whose grace continues to teach us how to love, forgive, give, and receive.

May we learn to give grace as freely as we hope to receive it.
