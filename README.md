# The Grace of Asking

**Learning to Give Grace Without Feeling Guilty for Receiving It**

A Christian teaching resource by **Bradley Hobbs** about the grace involved in asking for help, receiving with gratitude, giving freely, and respecting the boundaries that make healthy relationships possible.

## About the Book

Asking for help should not automatically make a person feel like a burden. At the same time, giving grace does not mean surrendering healthy boundaries or believing that every request must be fulfilled.

*The Grace of Asking* explores the balance between asking honestly, responding generously, receiving without unnecessary guilt, and allowing others the freedom to say yes or no. Its central message is that grace makes room for both the person who asks and the person who responds.

## The Eight Chapters

1. **Why Are We Always Sorry?**
2. **When Asking Feels Like a Burden**
3. **Can You Give Grace Without Giving It to Yourself?**
4. **Love Should Make Room for Asking**
5. **When Help Becomes an Expectation**
6. **The Freedom to Say Yes or No**
7. **Stop Apologizing for Being Human**
8. **Grace Goes Both Ways**

The book concludes with **You Are Not a Burden**.

## Scripture

The study materials use the **Amplified Bible, Classic Edition (AMPC)** for Scripture references and reflection. Scripture references are included throughout the chapter studies to support personal reading, discussion, and group study.
