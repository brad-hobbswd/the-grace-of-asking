About the book

Grace Must Make Room for Receiving

A reflection on the quiet guilt many people feel when they need something from someone else.

The Grace of Asking explores a familiar but rarely discussed struggle: the difficulty of asking for help without feeling guilty for needing it.

Some people are quick to show up for others, yet uncomfortable when someone offers to show up for them. They apologize before they ask. They minimize their needs. They worry that their request will inconvenience someone, even when the relationship is built on love.

This book considers what Scripture teaches us about compassion, humility, generosity, responsibility, and the freedom to say yes or no. Grace does not demand that every request be granted. It invites us to treat one another with dignity, honesty, and care.

You are allowed to have a need. Someone else is allowed to have a boundary. Love makes room for both.

The goal is not to create entitlement or guilt. It is to encourage relationships where people can give freely, receive gratefully, and speak honestly without shame.

Who this book is for

For the person who always says, “I’m sorry to bother you.” For the one who gives until they are exhausted. For anyone learning that healthy love includes both generosity and the humility to receive.
