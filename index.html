The Grace of Asking

Learning to Give Grace Without Feeling Guilty for Receiving It

Why can it feel easier to give help than to ask for it? This book invites us to consider the grace we extend to others, the grace we struggle to receive, and the love that makes room for both.

Read the IntroductionExplore the Chapters
The heart of this message

Grace was never meant to move in only one direction.

We often encourage people to be generous, patient, and understanding. But what happens when we are the ones who need help, patience, or understanding?

Giving Grace

Learning to respond to others with compassion instead of irritation or judgment.

Receiving Grace

Recognizing that having a need does not make a person a burden.

Living with Freedom

Making room for honest requests, healthy boundaries, and sincere love.
